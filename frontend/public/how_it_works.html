Our hybrid algorithm evaluates both content metrics (child age, special medical needs, caregiver skills, location, hourly budget) and collaborative filtering (parent rating patterns and satisfaction history) to calculate a personalized compatibility score.

Caregivers must provide a valid government-issued photo ID (Passport, Driver's License), criminal background check / police clearance, and optional professional certifications (CPR/First Aid, Early Childhood Education). All documents are manually reviewed by administrators.

Only parents with verified, completed bookings can submit reviews. Furthermore, our Machine Learning text classifier analyzes text patterns, repetitive phrases, and sentiment anomalies to flag suspicious reviews before they affect caregiver ratings.
