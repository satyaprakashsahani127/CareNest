How does CareNest AI calculate these scores?

CareNest uses a hybrid recommendation model combining Content-Based Filtering (calculating cosine similarity over child medical needs, allergy requirements, caregiver certifications, and geographic distance) with Collaborative Filtering (analyzing historical ratings from parents with similar child profiles). For new users or caregivers without prior reviews, the system automatically gives 100% weight to verified content attributes to resolve cold-start scenarios.
